### 3.3 Significance-marker convention

All v4.5.4-introduced figures use this convention consistently:

| p-value | Marker |
|---|---|
| p < 0.001 | `***` |
| p < 0.01 | `**` |
| p < 0.05 | `*` |
| p ≥ 0.05 | `ns` (or omitted) |
| missing | `n/a` |

Asterisks are placed:
- Forest plots: to the right of each 95% CI bar
- Scatter regressions: in the panel title after the gene/endpoint label
- Tabular Q1 Fisher-z slide: inline next to delta values

**Caveat**: existing v4.5.3 slides retain their original (pre-asterisk) appearance. Adding markers to all legacy slides was descoped from Round 3 because those figures are not regression-critical for the meeting; the convention is established and can be retroactively applied in a future round.

## 4. Three top headline findings from Round 3

### Finding 1 — The 50s decade carries the ADRB3-fibrosis signal (v4.5.4 Slide 29)

| Older-group definition | ADRB2 × fibrosis (subq F) | ADRB3 × fibrosis (subq F) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 50-79 (50s included) | **r = −0.43**, p<0.001 | r = −0.17, NS |
| 60-79 (50s excluded) | **r = −0.48**, p<0.001 | r = −0.06, NS |
| 50-59 only (peri window) | r = −0.37, p<0.01 | **r = −0.36**, p<0.01 |

**Interpretation**: ADRB2's fibrosis effect is robust across age slices and intensifies when 50s are removed. ADRB3's apparent broad-analysis effect is **driven entirely by the 50s decade** — it vanishes when the 50s are excluded. The two β-receptors track different time-courses through menopause: ADRB2 impairment is lasting; ADRB3 impairment may be acute-and-transient, peaking during the perimenopausal transition.

### Finding 2 — ADRB2 vs ADRB3 endpoint specialization (v4.5.4 Slide 43)

In female subq (n=233):

| Endpoint | ADRB2 R² | ADRB3 R² | Winner |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Age | 0.021 | **0.047** | ADRB3 |
| CCL2 | **0.090** | 0.027 | ADRB2 |
| Fibrosis | **0.174** | 0.026 | ADRB2 (large gap) |
| Senescence | 0.076 | **0.093** | ADRB3 (small gap) |

**Interpretation**: The two receptors are NOT redundant readouts of a single menopause-driven β-AR desensitization. ADRB2 dominates the inflammation/fibrosis axis; ADRB3 dominates the age/senescence axis. This argues for treating both receptors as separate manuscript dimensions rather than collapsing them into a "β-AR" composite.

### Finding 3 — Score-based stratification differs from age-bin stratification (v4.5.4 Slide 47)

The senMayoLike v2 transcriptional score divides females into tertiles that **partially but imperfectly align** with age-bin pre/peri/post grouping. About 20-25% of "pre" samples by age fall into the high-tertile (T3, "more post-like") and conversely. This means age-bin grouping under-resolves transcriptional state. For the manuscript:

- **Primary stratification**: age-bin pre/peri/post (existing, conventional)
- **Sensitivity stratification**: senMayoLike score tertiles (new in v4.5.4)
- **Concordance**: report agreement and document the discordant samples

The score-based approach also enables stratification within an age band: e.g., 40-49 women can be split by score tertile to reveal whether the apparent peri-menopausal effects are uniformly distributed or concentrated in transcriptionally-advanced women within the band.
