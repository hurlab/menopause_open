## 7. Recommendations for the meeting

1. **Lead with the v4.5.3 explainer PDF for context**: 21 pages, walks all 37 slides; the new "Revision history" block at the bottom has both rounds documented and signals to collaborators that the work has been independently audited twice.
2. **Slide 3 subtitle now contains the headline result**: "Depot × menopause interaction (cytokines): p = 0.003". A reviewer asking "what's the formal evidence for depot specificity?" can be pointed directly at the slide subtitle.
3. **Slide 4 conclusion is now precise about which window contains the female-specific result**: the 40-49 vs 60-69 window shown on the slide has parallel-aging signals; the female-specific TGFβ/ALK7 + CA-resistance signal is in the visceral 40-49 vs 50-59 peri-menopausal window (in the explainer detail).
4. **Slide 9 (glycolysis visceral)** wording explicitly states the depot contrast is statistical power, not direction reversal — useful when asked "why is visceral different".
5. **Slide 22** prose now lists the actual six gene/cell-type panels embedded in the slide. If asked why ADRB2 is not among the panels: ADRB2 cell-type-specific resolution was deferred to the next-steps follow-up (Slide 37).
6. **Slide 24 Adrb1** attribution is hedged — Kenichi can confirm whether the source is the figure panel on his Slide 1 or his email body.
7. **Slide 35** explicitly discloses the cytokine-vs-HALLMARK inflammation measure choice and clarifies that "(NS)" is a display annotation, not in the source TSV.
8. **Slide 36 Bullet 3** ADRB2 framing is hedged for the borderline female slope; interaction p=0.94 still rules out a sex-specific decline pattern.
9. **Slide 37** caveats now list both depot peri-menopausal sample sizes (subq 43/76; visc 36/54) and explicitly note that bin-midpoint attenuation does not invalidate p-values.
10. **Open follow-up**: per-cell-type ADRB2 vs fibrosis regression using the existing CIBERSORTx HiRes outputs is the natural next deliverable.
